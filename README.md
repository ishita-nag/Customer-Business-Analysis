Customer & Business Performance Analysis using Excel

# Project Overview

This project analyzes a simulated business dataset of 50 customer orders using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on revenue, profit, product performance, regional performance, customer segments, customer ratings, and monthly revenue trends.

# Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Analyze revenue and profit by region
- Compare customer types based on revenue contribution
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Track key business performance indicators
- Create actionable business insights and recommendations

# Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- Pivot-style analysis and formulas
- Data cleaning
- Charts and dashboard visualization

# Key Analysis

The project includes:

- Total customers and orders
- Total revenue and profit
- Profit margin
- Average customer rating
- Revenue by product category
- Profit by product category
- Revenue by region
- Profit by region
- Revenue by customer type
- Average rating by customer type
- Monthly revenue analysis

# Dashboard

The Excel dashboard provides a visual overview of:

- Monthly revenue trends
- Revenue by product category
- Profit by product category
- Revenue by region
- Profit by region
- Revenue by customer type
- Key business KPIs

# Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- Electronics also generated the highest profit.
- West was the highest-performing region based on revenue and profit.
- New customer records contributed higher total revenue than returning customer records in this dataset.

# Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Analyze regional performance to identify business opportunities.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends for business planning.
- Track customer ratings to maintain service quality.
- Compare customer segments to understand revenue contribution.

# Dataset

The dataset used in this project is simulated and created for portfolio and learning purposes. It contains 50 sample customer order records.

# Project Outcome

This project demonstrates practical skills in Excel-based data analysis, data organization, business reporting, dashboard creation, and converting data into business insights.
